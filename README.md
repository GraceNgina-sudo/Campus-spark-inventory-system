# ChainTrak: Smart Inventory & Supply Management for Chain Restaurants

## Project Overview

ChainTrak is a centralized inventory and supply management platform built specifically for chain restaurants. It transforms restaurant inventory data into actionable business intelligence, designed to address the unique challenges faced by Kenya and Africa's growing hospitality sector. The platform provides complete visibility over operations, aiming to reduce waste and improve profitability.

## The Challenge

Chain restaurants often grapple with significant operational problems that directly impact profitability. These include:

- **Food wastage** due to poor inventory visibility.

- **Theft and unexplained inventory losses**.

- **Manual, error-prone stock tracking**.

- **Overstocking and costly shortages**.

- **Poor coordination** between branches.

- Lack of **real-time monitoring or reporting**.

These issues lead to revenue loss, increased costs, delayed decisions, and a suboptimal customer experience.

## Key Features

ChainTrak offers a comprehensive suite of features to tackle these challenges:

- **Inventory Tracking:** Real-time stock levels, automatic updates, and reconciliation across all branches.

- **Smart Alerts:** Low-stock warnings, overstock notifications, and expiry tracking to minimize waste.

- **Multi-Branch Control:** Centralized visibility, branch-to-branch monitoring, and performance comparison.

- **Supplier Management:** Streamlined ordering, supplier records, and purchase tracking.

- **Reporting & Analytics:** Waste analysis, performance dashboards, and decision-support insights for better management.

## How It Works

ChainTrak connects every stage of the supply chain, from supplier delivery to the final sale, providing managers with a single, unified view of inventory operations. Every movement is tracked, recorded, and analyzed, eliminating blind spots and enabling faster, smarter decisions at every level of the chain.

## Market Opportunity & Business Model

ChainTrak targets growing hospitality businesses managing inventory across multiple locations, including fast-food franchises and hotel restaurant groups expanding across Kenya and East Africa. As chains expand, centralized inventory management becomes critical for maintaining profitability and operational consistency.

The business model is based on **SaaS Pricing Plans**:

| Plan | Branches | Price (KES/month) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Single Branch | 5,000 |
| Growth | 2–5 Branches | 12,000 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited Branches | Custom Pricing |

Additional revenue streams include onboarding, staff training, premium analytics, and technical support packages.
